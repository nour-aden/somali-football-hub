What happens in eleven minutes

We roast on a 12 kg drum and follow the curve above, adjusting heat by smell, sound and color as much as by the thermometer.

  1. 0:00

    Charge

    Green beans drop into a drum preheated to 200 °C. Bean temperature falls fast, then turns.

  2. 1:30

    Dry and brown

    The beans lose moisture and turn from green to yellow to tan. Sugars start to caramelize.

  3. 9:30

    First crack

    The beans pop as steam escapes. We lower the gas and slow the climb to protect the acidity.

  4. 11:00

    Drop and cool

    The batch falls into a cooling tray and reaches room temperature in four minutes.
